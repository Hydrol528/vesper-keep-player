Vesper Keep Player

Connect your own Jellyfin library, then browse and play it in the Keep’s VRChat theatre.

Before you begin

Install and set up Jellyfin Server with your movies or shows. Start Jellyfin Server before Vesper. You do not need the Jellyfin desktop player open. Vesper does not include Jellyfin or any movies.

Get Jellyfin Server · Jellyfin setup instructions

Every time you watch

  1. Make sure Jellyfin Server is running on the computer that holds your library.
  2. Open Vesper Keep Player from its desktop or Start menu shortcut. Wait for Ready, then click Open player.
  3. On the player page, click Connect Jellyfin. It shows a six-digit approval code and tries to copy it for you. If your Jellyfin Server is on a different computer, open the other-computer option and enter that server’s address first.
  4. Click Open Jellyfin. Sign in to your own Jellyfin account if asked. Open your profile menu at the top-right, then Quick Connect. Paste the code and approve it.
  5. Return to the Vesper page. Your library appears automatically. Click Pair my in-world menu, then Copy full link.
  6. In VRChat, join the Keep and go to the theatre. Click the small round logo below the screen. Open the Jellyfin tab and click its pairing field.
  7. Paste your copied link into the Enter URL box. Submit with the blue arrow, then choose Connect library.
  8. Choose a movie, or a series, season and episode, then press Play.

Keep Jellyfin Server and Vesper Keep Player running while watching. After approval you may close the Jellyfin browser tab. Use Stop pairing to remove catalogue access, or Stop and exit in the Vesper app when finished.

Where is it installed?

The installer shows your installation folder and lets you change it. The app, its bundled runtime and its temporary Data folder stay inside that installation folder. Choose a folder you can write to. Vesper does not move your movies or change Jellyfin’s library folders.

Uninstall

Close Vesper with Stop and exit. Open Windows Settings → Apps → Installed apps → Vesper Keep Player → Uninstall. This removes Vesper, its shortcuts and temporary data. Jellyfin and your movies are left alone.

What is shared?

After you pair, people in that Keep instance can browse the shared catalogue and choose what to play, subject to the screen controls. Leaving the Keep or choosing Stop pairing ends catalogue access. Your Jellyfin password is never entered into Vesper. Temporary access is held in memory, not saved as a login.

Current testing limits

This preview is for Windows PC playback. Movies hosted only on your home network are not yet available to viewers outside that network or a standalone Quest through this local setup. Remote playback is still being developed.
